Player consolidation · proposal for review

One player, not four

The archive currently runs four independent audio players — the show-page track rows, the “Full Recording” player, /playlist/, and the /player/ popup — each with its own markup, its own state, and its own gaps. This mocks up a single component instead, in the three densities the site actually needs, plus the loudness control that started this conversation.

What's running today

4 independent <audio> elements

Coordinated only by a BroadcastChannel claim/pause protocol so two don't play at once — not a shared component. Each has its own play button, its own progress bar, its own idea of what a track is.

The loudness control, up close

the feature that started this

Not a remaster — a live, client-side gain stage guarded against overload, labeled by intent (Archive/Louder/Loudest) rather than a promised output number, since a fixed boost doesn't land on the same LUFS for every track.

Playback loudness

Archive (as mastered)+0 dB
Louder+4 dB
Loudest+6 dB
A per‑listener gain stage with overload protection underneath — the stored FLAC/MP3 never changes. Exact loudness still varies by track; this sets intent, not a guaranteed number.

Archive plays exactly what's on R2 — the linear‑gain, dynamics‑preserving master the whole pipeline exists to protect. This mode bypasses any dynamics processing entirely.

Louder / Loudest apply a client‑side GainNode against overload protection, so a listener on a quiet device can turn a track up without touching the master file. Getting a true no-clip guarantee (not just a compressor's threshold) is open engineering work, tracked in the plan.

3 · Mini bar

replaces /playlist/'s queue player

Same component again, collapsed to a footer strip — sticky within the current page while something plays. This does not survive clicking to another page on its own: the site is a static multi-page site, so a full navigation still tears down playback. The /player/ popup remains the practical workaround for uninterrupted listening while browsing until site-wide sticky navigation is separately decided (see the plan doc's open questions).

Same controls, everywhere

one component, three densities

What each density shows or hides:

1
Play / pauseAlways present. Only size changes.
2
Waveform seekFull-width in the hero, a thin bar in the row, hidden in the mini bar.
3
Loudness pillSame control, same three options, in all three densities.
4
RepeatHero only — a row is too narrow, and repeating a whole track is a hero-level intent.
5
DownloadRow and hero. Dropped from the mini bar — that's a destination, not a while-browsing action.
6
Prev / nextMini bar and hero, wherever a queue exists (playlist, popup, autoplay-next tracklists).

Other functions on the table

for review, not yet built

Share timestampproposed
Copies a link that opens straight to the current second — the kind of thing worth pointing someone at a specific moment for, e.g. a review-tier transient-cap flag.
Repeatproposed
Restarts the current track on end instead of advancing the queue — a plain repeat-one toggle, not a loop-region editor. Fits a live tape better than scrubbing a passage.
Keyboard shortcutsproposed
space play/pause, ←/→ seek ±5s, ↑/↓ next/prev in queue — once there's one component, one set of bindings can live everywhere instead of nowhere.
Loudness (this doc)headline feature
Archive / Louder / Loudest via a client-side GainNode + limiter. Doesn't touch the master files; sidesteps the true-peak ceiling entirely instead of remastering into it.
