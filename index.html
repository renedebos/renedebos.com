About This Archive

These recordings were captured on a Sony portable DAT recorder at clubs in Marin and the Bay Area in the late 1990s and early 2000s. Audio quality varies — the tapes have aged, the microphone was small, and some shows had significant background noise. They are presented here as-is.

The larger lossless WAV files are password protected. In the future I plan to make smaller compressed MP3 versions freely available, and to do the work of parsing through the tapes properly: identifying tracks, adding song titles, and putting together some curated playlists.

Most recordings are large lossless WAV files (1–3 GB) and may take a moment to begin playing. Track listings and smaller MP3 versions are coming in the future.
