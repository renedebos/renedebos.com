About This Show

Recorded from the audience on DAT at 19 Broadway in Fairfax on August 23, 1999 — a 14-song solo set. Part 1 of the tape exists but is too heavily distorted to use, so the show is drawn entirely from the surviving Part 2 reel.

Mostly originals, including an early version of “Rugburns” before it found its familiar shape, alongside covers of Steve Poltz’s “Single Life,” Bruce Springsteen’s “Highway Patrolman,” Lefty Frizzell’s “Long Black Veil,” and Paddy Nash’s “Model Family Man.”

Straightforward hand-edits — split, faded, and clip-repaired — then normalized to the archive’s −20 LUFS standard. No noise reduction or EQ needed on this tape.
