About This Show

Recorded from the audience on DAT at 19 Broadway in Fairfax on November 15, 1999 — a shorter, fourteen-song solo set.

Notable for an early version of "Burning Man," long before the song found its familiar shape, and a short alternate-verse take of "Hollywood" played right after the full version.

The show has been split into individual songs and loudness-normalized to an even listening level, with standard fades and minor clip repairs done by hand; no noise reduction was applied.
