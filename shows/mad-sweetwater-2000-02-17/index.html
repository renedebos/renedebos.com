About This Show

This Mad Hannans set at the Sweetwater features a few guest singers. A 14-year-old (!) Kristen McNamara sings “Plastic Lemons,” “Elephant Shoes,” and “Everything Reminds Me of You,” and an unidentified singer takes “Angel from Montgomery.”

The songs have not been normalized in Audacity yet — that will be done later.
