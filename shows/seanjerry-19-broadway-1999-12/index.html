About This Show

A fun show with both Jerry and Sean performing but the recording has quite a significant amount of background chatter from the bar. Jerry has some voice issues during this time and most of the vocals are done by Sean. This concert stands out for a very early version of 'Perfect Autumn Day'. You can find the full finished version of this song at https://jerryhannan.com/sean-s-page.
