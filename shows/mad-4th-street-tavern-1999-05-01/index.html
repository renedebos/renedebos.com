About This Show

Recorded from the audience on DAT at 4th Street Tavern in San Rafael on May 1, 1999.

The show has been split into individual songs and loudness-normalized to an even listening level, with the applause faded down at the end of each track for easier streaming, and some minor clipping repaired by hand. The set opens with a soundcheck.
