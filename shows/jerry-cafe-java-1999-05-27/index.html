About This Show

Recorded from the audience on DAT at Cafe Java in Larkspur on May 27, 1999 — a 20-song solo set mostly Hannan originals, with covers of John Prine’s “Illegal Smile” and “Please Don’t Bury Me,” and Paddy Nash’s “Model Family Man,” alongside a pair of Irish traditionals.

The show has been split into individual songs and loudness-normalized to an even listening level.

The set closes with the traditional “The Parting Glass.”
