Jerry Hannan
Live at Cafe Java

Larkspur, California · 1999-04-08 · Audience recording

Highlight show

About This Show

Recorded from the audience on DAT at Cafe Java in Larkspur on April 8, 1999 — a 23-song solo set of Hannan originals with covers of Cat Stevens, Bruce Springsteen, Lefty Frizzell, John Prine, and Van Morrison, closing with the Irish traditional “The Parting Glass.” Kelly Peterson joins Jerry for “Daddy.”

This is the second date on the March 25 tape, split out as its own show; a separate digital transfer of the April 8 set is also available as a full-recording download.

The Eagles' “Peaceful Easy Feeling” makes its first appearance in the archive and has Kelly Peterson on main vocals. Kelly is also on Angel from Montgomery and her own song Daddy.
