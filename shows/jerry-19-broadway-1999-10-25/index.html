About This Show

Recorded from the audience on DAT at 19 Broadway in Fairfax on October 25, 1999 — a long, twenty-six-song solo set.

Kelly Peterson joins as a guest vocalist on two covers, John Prine’s “Angel of Montgomery” and the Eagles’ “Peaceful Easy Feeling”; the set also includes an alternate take of “ABC” and an early version of “Hollywood.”

The whole tape got a pass of broadband noise reduction (6dB, sensitivity 5) in Audacity before the tracks were split, then the usual loudness normalization to the archive standard.
