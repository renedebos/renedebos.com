About This Show

Recorded from the audience on DAT at Cafe Java in Larkspur on May 10, 1999 — a 21-song solo set of Hannan originals alongside covers of Bruce Springsteen (“Highway Patrolman,” “Born to Run”), three John Prine songs, Paddy Nash, and the Irish traditional “The Patriot Game.”

The show has been split into individual songs and loudness-normalized to an even listening level.

The set closes with Peter Jones' “Kilkelly, Ireland,” a story-song about a family split by emigration.
