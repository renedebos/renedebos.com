About This Show

The first Jerry Hannan show recorded at 19 Broadway — the tape has quite a few significant dropouts that can’t be fully fixed, affecting Candle in the Wind, Angel from Montgomery, and Blind Man, with Sean Hannan sitting in for a few songs. Where possible the dropouts were cut from the song and hopefully do not cause too much of a nuisance; Blind Man’s gap (around 3:10) is still rough and flagged for a closer look.

Perfect Autumn Day appears twice in the set, still a work in progress at this early stage.
