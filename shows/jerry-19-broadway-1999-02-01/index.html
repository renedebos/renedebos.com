About This Show

The first Jerry Hannan show recorded at 19 Broadway — the tape has some significant dropouts, affecting a couple of tracks (Candle in the Wind, Angel of Montgomery), with Sean Hannan sitting in for a few songs. Where possible the dropouts were cut from the song and hopefully do not cause too much of a nuisance.

Perfect Autumn Day appears twice in the set, still a work in progress at this early stage.

Broadband noise reduction (noise reduction = 6dB, sensitivity = 5) was applied to the full recording in Audacity before the tracks were split, and the individual tracks were then loudness-normalized to −20 LUFS.
