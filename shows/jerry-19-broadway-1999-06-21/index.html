About This Show

One of the first and early solo 19 Broadway shows — a loud and rowdy night in Fairfax.

The individual tracks have been loudness-normalized to even out the different sections of this tape, which were recorded at different microphone input levels (the change comes around the 45-minute mark). This is the first show on the site to be processed through the new automated loudness-normalization workflow. A loudness-normalized version of the whole show is also available below as a single lossless FLAC.
