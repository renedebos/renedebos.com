About This Show

A marathon early solo night at 19 Broadway in Fairfax — 34 songs mixing Hannan originals with a run of John Prine covers, two Cat Stevens songs, and a closing stretch of Irish traditionals.

Worthwhile note is the appearance yet again of Perfect Autumn Day.

Broadband noise reduction (noise reduction = 6dB, sensitivity = 5) was applied to the full recording in Audacity before the tracks were split, a first for the archive; and the individual tracks were then loudness-normalized to the archive's standard listening level. Fades and minor clip repairs were done by hand as usual.
