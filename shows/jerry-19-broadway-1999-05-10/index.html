About This Show

Recorded from the audience on DAT at 19 Broadway in Fairfax on May 10, 1999 — one of Jerry's early solo nights at the club.

The show has been split into individual songs and loudness-normalized to an even listening level, with the applause faded down at the end of each track for easier streaming.

This show includes 'Jerry is Gone(?)'. Not sure if this is the correct title and not positive if this song has ever been recorded.
