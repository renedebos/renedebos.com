About This Show

Kelly Peterson sings two songs on this night — “Daddy” and “Angel from Montgomery.” “Daddy” is a Kelly Peterson original; you can find her on Facebook at @KellyPetersonBand.

Jerry Hannan also sings a few tunes here, with Sean on percussion.

Processing note: these tracks were processed in Audacity with loudness normalization to −16 LUFS, recorded here to keep track of the changes made to the songs.
