Mad Hannans
Live at Marin Brewing Co.

Larkspur, California · 1999-04-01 · Audience recording

About This Show

The Mad Hannans back at Marin Brewing Co. in Larkspur exactly one year after their first show there, on the venue's 10th anniversary. Two audience transfers of the tape survive; the tracks here are split from the cleaner of the two, with the other kept below as an alternate full-show download. “Da Da Da (Slave to an Angel)” now opens with its “The Kiss” intro, missing a year earlier.
