About This Show

Unlike the audience tapes that make up most of this archive, this February 2000 set at 19 Broadway was recorded straight off the soundboard — so it’s unusually clean and close, with Sean’s voice and guitar right up front.

The tape has been split into its 11 songs — Sean’s originals alongside a few traditional Irish tunes and covers. Each track streams here as a 320 kbps MP3, or can be downloaded as a lossless FLAC (password protected).

In all this is a relatively short set for Sean. The tracks — both the streaming MP3s and the lossless FLAC files — have been loudness-normalized to −16 LUFS for a more even, consistent listening level.

On “Plastic Lemons” it is actually Jerry Hannan singing — he stepped in for a song while Sean, who was quite tired this night, took a break.

The full tape also includes some guitar music from an unidentified musician, and has not yet been fully audited.
