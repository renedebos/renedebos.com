About This Show

This show was a very muddled-sounding recording. The tracks have been restored with corrective EQ — cutting the low-mid mud and lifting presence and air to bring the vocals forward — and loudness-normalized to −20 LUFS to match the rest of the archive. Still not pristine, but a clear improvement over the raw tape transfer.
