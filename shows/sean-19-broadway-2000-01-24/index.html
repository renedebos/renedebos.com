About This Show

19 Broadway is a small, famously loud bar in Fairfax, and this night was no exception — the room was packed and talking straight through the set yet at times fully engaged. The tape was captured from the audience on a DAT recorder, so you’ll hear the crowd, the clink of glasses, and the bar’s heavy front door banging open and shut between songs.

Despite the racket, Sean played a long, generous set. The full tape has been split into its 31 individual songs — Sean’s originals alongside traditional tunes and a handful of covers.

“Model Family Man” was originally written by the Hannans’ friend Paddy Nash in Ireland.
